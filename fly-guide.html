About the Fly Guide

This page will provide information about different fly patterns used for trout fishing in Western Pennsylvania.

Types of Flies

The guide will eventually include dry flies, nymphs, wet flies, emergers, and streamers.

Each pattern can include information such as its name, size, color, what it imitates, and the situations in which it can be effective.

Helpful Tips for Fly Anglers

  • Match your fly patterns to the aquatic insects present in the stream.
  • Pay attention to water temperature and stream conditions when planning a fishing trip.
  • Use natural presentations and avoid excessive drag when fishing dry flies.
  • Explore different fly patterns throughout the fishing season.
  • Practice proper catch-and-release techniques to help protect trout populations.
