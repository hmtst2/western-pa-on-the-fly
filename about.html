About Me

Welcome to Western PA on the Fly! I created this website to share my passion for fly fishing and the experiences I've had exploring trout streams throughout Western Pennsylvania.

Fly fishing has become one of my favorite outdoor activities because it combines time spent in nature with the challenge of understanding trout behavior, aquatic insects, and different fishing techniques.

Living in Western Pennsylvania provides opportunities to explore a variety of streams, creeks, and rivers that support trout populations.

Life on the Water

One of the things I enjoy most about fly fishing is the opportunity to experience different streams and spend time outdoors.

Why I Enjoy Fly Fishing

Fly fishing is different from many other forms of fishing because success often depends on understanding the natural environment.

Identifying insects, selecting the appropriate fly pattern, and making a natural presentation all contribute to the experience.

I also enjoy the patience and attention to detail that fly fishing requires. Every trip offers an opportunity to learn something new, whether it's discovering a new fishing location or improving a particular technique.

Beyond catching fish, fly fishing provides an opportunity to appreciate the streams, wildlife, and natural landscapes of Western Pennsylvania.

Catch and Release

Practicing responsible catch and release is an important part of protecting trout populations and preserving fishing opportunities.

When releasing trout, anglers should minimize handling, keep fish in the water whenever possible, and avoid unnecessary stress.

About This Website

Western PA on the Fly was developed as a student web design project for CSIT 260: Web Programming I at Mount Aloysius College.

The website combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, photographs, and videos to create an informational resource about fly fishing.

My goal was to create a website that reflects my personal interests while demonstrating the web development skills learned throughout the course.
