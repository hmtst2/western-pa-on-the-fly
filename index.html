About This Website

Western PA on the Fly is a website dedicated to fly fishing in Western Pennsylvania. The site combines my personal experiences on the water with information about trout, fly patterns, aquatic insects, fishing techniques, equipment, and the seasonal changes that affect fly fishing throughout the year.

Fly Fishing in Western Pennsylvania

Western Pennsylvania offers a wide variety of streams and fishing opportunities for fly anglers. This website serves as both a personal fly fishing journal and an educational resource based on what I learn and observe while spending time on the water.

Visit the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission for fishing regulations and other information about fishing in Pennsylvania.
