Welcome to Western PA on the Fly

Welcome to Western PA on the Fly, a website dedicated to the art and experience of fly fishing in Western Pennsylvania.

From the mountain streams of the Alleghenies to the rivers and creeks throughout the region, Western Pennsylvania offers opportunities to pursue trout in beautiful natural environments.

This website shares my personal experiences on the water while introducing visitors to fly fishing techniques, artificial fly patterns, and aquatic insects.
